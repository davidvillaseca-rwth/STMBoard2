Analog finder: A0 to A5

Flash the analog-finder firmware, click Connect and choose the STLink port. Click Set baseline with nothing near the shield, then try: finger on LED1 and the square part above it, covering the area with something dark, and shining a phone flashlight on it. The pin that changes most is outlined in orange. "Change" is relative to the baseline. A floating (unconnected) pin wanders and looks noisy; a pin tied to a circuit sits steady.
